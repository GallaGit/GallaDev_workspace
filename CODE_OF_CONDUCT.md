# Code of Conduct

> **Español:** [Código de conducta en español](./CODE_OF_CONDUCT.es.md)

## Our pledge

We pledge to make participation in GallaDev Workspace a harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of age, body size, disability, ethnicity, gender identity and expression, level of experience, nationality, personal appearance, race, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

## Our standards

Positive behavior includes: using welcoming and inclusive language, being respectful of differing viewpoints, gracefully accepting constructive criticism, focusing on what is best for the project, and showing empathy toward other contributors.

Unacceptable behavior includes: sexualized language or imagery, trolling, insulting or derogatory comments, personal or political attacks, public or private harassment, publishing others' private information without permission, or any conduct that would reasonably be considered inappropriate in a professional setting.

## Scope

This Code applies in all project spaces (repository, issues, pull requests, discussions) and whenever an individual represents the project in public.

## Enforcement

Violations may result in a warning, temporary ban, or permanent expulsion from the project, at the maintainers' discretion.

Reports: open a confidential report via [GitHub Security Advisories](https://github.com/GallaGit/GallaDev_workspace/security/advisories/new) (mark it as a Code of Conduct matter, not a vulnerability) or contact the repository owner directly through a private channel. All reports are handled confidentially.
